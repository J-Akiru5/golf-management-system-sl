Drawing a day, step by step

Draw one playing day at a time. Before you press the draw button, every player must be confirmed and their handicap index frozen. Do it in that order and the Checks list stays empty.

Freeze first. Then draw. Never the other way round.

The draw groups players by class, and the class comes from the frozen index. If the index changes after the draw, the groups no longer match the players. That is what produced the 343 checks.

Before you draw: the checklist

Tick these off. Your ticks stay on this device. All six must be true before you press the draw button.

Changed an index after drawing? Freeze again, then redraw that day. Do not try to fix the groups by hand one at a time.

Draw one day

  1. Open Draw & pairingsPick the day under "Day of the event".
    Draw & pairings
  2. Press "Make the draw"The board fills with groups for that day only. Later you may see it called "Re-run proposal (keeps locks)". It does the same thing.
    Draw & pairings, top of the page
  3. Read the four tilesReady players in a group should say the same number twice, like 53/53. "Must fix" should be 0. "To check" should be 0 or a few lines you understand.
    Tiles under the day picker
  4. Open the Checks tabIf there are checks, read the cards at the top first. They say what is behind the numbers and give the next step.
    Checks button, under the tiles
  5. Lock the groups you are happy withA locked group keeps its players when you redraw. Use it for groups the organizers asked for by name.
    Lock button on each group

Moving a player by hand

Tap a player, then tap the group. A move that puts someone in a full or locked group is refused and says why. Choose a reason from "Override reason" first so the log shows why.

Why 343? Last time only 2 of 283 players had been frozen. That gave 281 lines of "Re-freeze", and the draw had been made before the indexes arrived, which gave 61 more lines for groups that no longer matched. Two causes. Freezing and redrawing clears both.

Publish and print

  1. Press "Review and publish"The preview lists every group. Check the day, the times and the holes.
    Draw & pairings
  2. Press "Publish N groups"Players see only this frozen copy, never the live board. Publishing Day 2 keeps Day 1 as it was.
    Publish window
  3. Set the tee timesOpen Tee times, pick the day, press "Take the draw's times" and confirm each group.
    Tee times
  4. Print the sheetsChoose "Day to print", then print the start sheet, pairings and scorecards.
    Print center

If you change the board after publishing, the page shows that it has changed since publish. Publish again when you are ready. Players keep seeing the old copy until you do.

Then go back to step 5 and draw the next day.

Five rules to remember

  1. Freeze before you draw.
  2. One day at a time.
  3. If an index changes, freeze and redraw. Do not patch groups.
  4. Red must be zero before you publish.
  5. Check the day before you press publish.
