# Bug notes, 10 Nov 2026

For the club and the tournament desk. Plain language, no technical detail.

How to read this: "Fixed" means the change is built and in the app. "Open" means it still needs
work or a decision. These notes come from the issue list and the project records. Nobody has
re-tested every item on the live site today, so anything marked "check on the live site" should be
tried by someone at the desk.

## Fixed in this round

**Printed scorecards no longer say "Draft".**
The scorecard download (PDF) used to print "Draft · not published" at the bottom of every page.
That wording belongs to the pairings list, and it showed on scorecards even when the course
numbers were already confirmed. Scorecards now print "Course data confirmed" when the course
numbers have been confirmed in Settings. If the numbers are not confirmed, they say
"Course data not confirmed" or "Course data not entered", so a card that is not ready still warns
you. The same label shows on the Print screen when you open the Scorecards tab. The start sheet
and the pairings sheet keep their own "Draft · not published" wording, because that is about
whether the pairings are published.

## Fixed earlier (already in the app)

- **Player names on the Scores screen could not be clicked (issue 170).** Names now open the
  player's record.
- **Alerts could not be marked as read (issue 171).** Each alert has a Mark as read button, there
  is a Mark all as read button, and the alert panel closes when you move to another page.
- **The back button on the published pairings page went to the wrong place (issue 207).** It now
  returns to where you came from.
- **Public pages:** the home page shows the venue instead of the tee time, both partners stay on
  the registration card when you search for one of them, and the pairings page updates by itself
  when a new version is published.

## Open: things the desk will notice

1. **Alerts still staying on screen after "Mark as read" (issue 197).** This was reported again
   after the fix above. Check on the live site. If it still happens, tell us which alert and which
   screen.
2. **A player's handicap cannot be changed after the event is closed (issue 204).** In real life
   corrections happen after closing, so the desk needs to be able to fix a handicap and keep a
   record of who changed it and why. Not done yet.
3. **Printed pairings heading (issue 202).** The heading should say "Member and Guest" for a pair.
   A player with no partner should be marked Member or Guest only. Not done yet.
4. **Pairing board is hard to read (issue 203).** Each player should show whether they are a
   member or a guest, and the two players of a pair should share the same background colour. Not
   done yet.
5. **Help and Control Room screen layout (issue 172).** The layout looks wrong when the Help
   button, the Control Room and the "Show me" button on the Readiness check are used together.
   Not confirmed fixed.
6. **Scanned scorecards before 12 Nov (issue 149).** A scanned card can only be saved once the
   round has been opened from the published draw on the Scores screen. The button for this is
   built. Someone must open the round for each playing day before the first card is scanned, and
   try one test card.
7. **Scanning without internet (issue 162).** The camera, the card guide and Upload are in place.
   The reading of the card still needs a working internet connection. At the course, keep the
   paper cards as the record and scan when there is a connection.
8. **Clubhouse TV display (issue 152).** The big-screen player view only works on a computer that
   is signed in to the desk. A TV that is not signed in shows "This player is not on the desk".
   Showing names and scores on a screen without a sign-in is a club decision.
9. **Who can use the player side (issue 164).** Today a player needs an NGAP number and to be
   verified before seeing their own pages. The proposal is that any signed-in player can see their
   own entry and the published list, and the NGAP number is optional. This waits for the club's
   answer.
