Fixed this round: printed scorecards no longer say “Draft”

FIXED

The problem

The scorecard download (PDF) printed “Draft · not published” at the bottom of every page, even when the course numbers were already confirmed. That wording is about the pairings list. It should never have appeared on a scorecard.

A scorecard now shows whether the course numbers are confirmed, because that is what a scorecard depends on.

Course numbers in SettingsFooter on every scorecard page
ConfirmedCourse data confirmed
Entered but not confirmedCourse data not confirmed
Not enteredCourse data not entered

The Print screen shows the same label when you open the Scorecards tab. An unready card still warns you. The start sheet and the pairings sheet keep “Draft · not published”, because for those it is true: it means the pairings have not been published yet.

Needs a fix

ISSUE 197

Alerts still staying on screen after “Mark as read”

This was reported again after the earlier fix (issue 171). Check on the live site. If it still happens, tell us which alert and which screen.

ISSUE 204

A player’s handicap cannot be changed after the event is closed

In real life corrections happen after closing. The desk needs to fix a handicap and keep a record of who changed it and why. Not done yet.

ISSUE 202

Printed pairings heading

The heading should say “Member and Guest” for a pair. A player with no partner should be marked Member or Guest only. Not done yet.

ISSUE 203

The pairing board is hard to read

Each player should show whether they are a member or a guest, and the two players of a pair should share the same background colour. Not done yet.

ISSUE 172

Help and Control Room screen layout

The layout looks wrong when the Help button, the Control Room and the “Show me” button on the Readiness check are used together. Not confirmed fixed.

Needs a step by the desk before 12 November

ISSUE 149

Scanned scorecards need the round opened first

A scanned card can only be saved once the round has been opened from the published draw on the Scores screen. The button for this is built.

The desk must open the round for each playing day, then try one test card, before the first real card is scanned.

Waiting for a club decision

ISSUE 162

Scanning without internet

The camera, the card guide and Upload are in place. Reading the card still needs a working internet connection. At the course, keep the paper cards as the record and scan when there is a connection.

ISSUE 152

Clubhouse TV display

The big-screen player view only works on a computer signed in to the desk. A TV that is not signed in shows “This player is not on the desk”. Showing names and scores on a screen with no sign-in is a club decision.

ISSUE 164

Who can use the player side

Today a player needs an NGAP number and to be verified before seeing their own pages. The proposal: any signed-in player can see their own entry and the published list, and the NGAP number is optional. This waits for the club’s answer.
