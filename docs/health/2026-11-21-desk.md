## 1. Verdict for v1.0.0

**Ready with fixes.** The desk's core is sound and the suite is green: one day is drawn with its own
sessions from the stored day times, a save names its day, a failed save puts the previous board back,
and publishing keeps every day. Four things should be fixed or verified first: the start sheet files
an 11:30 afternoon under AM (F1), re-freezing an overridden index leaves the old playing handicap
behind (F2), the app's playing-handicap rounding differs from the scoring function at allowances
below 100% (F3), and the plan and the changelog disagree about whether the per-day round migration is
applied (F4). No P0 was found in the desk code.

## 4. What works (do not touch)

- **The draw is day-aware and reproducible.** One day is routed from that day's entries, with each
  player's own AM/PM, from that day's stored start times; the same field draws the same holes (a
  seed from the field). Locked groups are held and released with a reason when they stop making sense.
  Evidence: `src/lib/store/state.ts:439-527`, `src/lib/routing/index.ts:313-424`, `:476-483`.
- **Publishing is per day and frozen.** Players only ever read the published snapshot, each day
  compares with its own published copy, and a takedown only happens after the database confirms it.
  Evidence: `src/lib/store/state.ts:542-583`, `src/lib/store/desk-store.tsx:913-961`.
- **A refused write does not leave a wrong screen.** Setup, index edits, re-freeze, tee assignment,
  days, caddies and the notification bell either write first or put the previous value back; a failed
  draw save restores the old board and blocks a live publish. Evidence: `adapters.ts:1686-1723`,
  `desk-store.tsx:943-961`.
- **The handicap arithmetic at 100% matches the club's tables,** including the 30 cap and the plus
  handicap direction, with the printed-table values pinned in tests.
  Evidence: `src/lib/routing/handicap.ts:1-51`; `src/lib/routing/course-handicap-table.test.ts`
  (3 cases against the printed tables) and `playing-cap.test.ts` (6 cases on the cap).
- **A tee is taken from the registration, not the category, when the two disagree,** and a missing or
  unknown tee is never swapped for another one. Evidence: `src/lib/data/registrations.ts:77-104`.
- **Class beats the tee, and a pair is classed on both partners.** Evidence:
  `src/lib/routing/classify.ts:72-136`; `src/lib/store/state.ts:410-414`.
- **Unverifiable players are never given a default.** An entry the engine cannot classify is listed
  blocking on the board, and a confirmed but incomplete entry goes to an Unclassified group with the
  missing facts named. Evidence: `src/lib/routing/index.ts:118-162`, `:426-465`;
